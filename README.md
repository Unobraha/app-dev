# Spirited Away: Courage Beyond Fear

## A. Introduction

**Spirited Away** is a Japanese animated fantasy film released in **2001**. It follows *Chihiro*, a young girl who enters a mysterious world of spirits after her parents are transformed. The movie explores courage, responsibility, and the importance of staying true to oneself.

---

## B. Synopsis

1. Chihiro and her parents discover a mysterious place while traveling to their new home.
2. Her parents eat food that belongs to the spirits and are transformed into pigs.
3. A mysterious boy named Haku helps Chihiro survive in the spirit world.
4. Chihiro takes a job at a bathhouse controlled by the witch Yubaba.
5. Yubaba renames Chihiro as *Sen* and takes away part of her identity.
6. Sen must work hard, make new friends, and find a way to save her parents.
7. Through her journey, Chihiro becomes braver and more independent.

## C. Characters

- **Chihiro Ogino / Sen:** The main character, a young girl who learns courage and responsibility.
- **Haku:** A mysterious boy who helps Chihiro navigate the spirit world.
- **Yubaba:** The powerful witch who controls the bathhouse and gives Chihiro a new name.
- **No-Face:** A lonely spirit whose behavior becomes dangerous when he enters the bathhouse.
- **Lin:** A bathhouse worker who helps Chihiro understand her new environment.
- **Zeniba:** Yubaba’s twin sister, who plays an important role in Chihiro’s journey.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose *Spirited Away* because I enjoy imaginative stories that show how a person can grow through difficult experiences. The movie is both entertaining and meaningful because it teaches lessons about courage, kindness, and responsibility.

### Three Things I Liked

1. **The animation:** The detailed backgrounds and creative spirit designs make the world feel magical.
2. **Chihiro’s growth:** I like how she changes from a scared girl into a brave and determined character.
3. **The emotional story:** The movie combines adventure with important lessons about family and identity.

> **My favorite message:** Courage does not mean having no fear; it means doing what is right even when you are afraid.

### Rating and Recommendation

**My rating: 9/10**

I would recommend *Spirited Away* to people who enjoy animated movies, fantasy adventures, and stories with strong characters. Some scenes may feel strange or scary for younger viewers, so it is best watched with family or friends.
